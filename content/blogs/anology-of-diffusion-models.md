## Discriminative vs Generative Models

| | **Discriminative model** | **Generative model** |
|---|---|---|
| **Goal** | Directly estimate \( P(y \| x) \) | Estimate \( P(x \| y) \) |
| **What's learned** | Decision boundary | Probability distributions of the data |
