An AI-powered art generation app that turns pet photos into beautiful artwork for your home. Upload a photo of your pet and choose from a variety of artistic styles — the app generates print-ready portraits and decorations you can order directly.

## What It Does

- **Pet portrait generation** — transform any pet photo into stylized artwork (oil painting, watercolor, pop art, and more)
- **Home decoration focus** — outputs are sized and formatted for printing as wall art, canvas prints, and framed pieces
- **Style customization** — choose artistic styles, color palettes, and compositions tailored to your space
